Stopping churn before it starts

I managed a $3M book of business where accounts were churning quietly — the kind you only notice at renewal. I built a Health Check program: regular education and platform tune-ups that reached accounts before they were at risk.

The call I made: run it on a cadence — education and tune-ups up front, instead of rescue missions at renewal time.

30% churn reduction in one year

The limit: it only worked where someone on the client side engaged. No program fixes an empty chair.

Principles

What I believe.

Adoption is the product.

If they don’t use it, you didn’t ship it. I learned this watching renewals die on unused licenses.

Teach, don’t tell.

Documentation nobody reads is the same as no documentation. If the team can’t explain it back, the rollout isn’t done.

Define good in numbers first.

If we can’t agree what good adoption looks like before kickoff, we’ll argue about it after.

AI where it earns its keep.

If it can’t point to a number it moved, it was a demo, not a deployment. No hype, no pilots to nowhere.
