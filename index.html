Selected work

Two times it worked.

01
Docebo · 2022

Stopping churn before it starts

A $3M book of business was churning quietly — nobody saw it coming. I built a proactive Health Check program: user education and platform optimization that reached accounts before they were at risk, not after.

The call I made: education over discounts. I taught admins to run the platform themselves instead of papering over risk with concessions.

30% churn reduction in one year

The limit: it worked where an admin would show up. Dark accounts needed a different play.

02
ADP · Contract

Migrations without the mess

Consultants were losing hours to migration errors, and record accuracy in Monday.com kept slipping. I set up a weekly enablement cadence — lunch-and-learns, open office hours — and tightened the record-keeping workflows behind it.

The call I made: fix the people system before the data system. Training the consultants beat adding another layer of validation rules.

20% fewer migration errors over six months

The limit: six months of steady cadence. There’s no shortcut version of this.

Principles

What I believe.

Adoption is the product.

If they don’t use it, you didn’t ship it. I learned this watching renewals die on unused licenses.

Teach, don’t tell.

Documentation nobody reads is the same as no documentation. If the team can’t explain it back, the rollout isn’t done.

Define good in numbers first.

If we can’t agree what good adoption looks like before kickoff, we’ll argue about it after.

AI where it earns its keep.

Into training, support, and content — where it works. Kept out of where it doesn’t. No hype, no pilots to nowhere.
